The chain-level insight

The work is identical for everyone; only the price point and the localisation differ.

Every incumbent — PwC, KPMG, S&P, Uniqus — runs this same eight-step flow. So the workflow is not a differentiator; it's table stakes. The two places value actually concentrates are (1) the hard, manual, UAE-specific data & calculation steps (district cooling, local factors, free-zone boundaries) that generic SaaS gets wrong, and (2) the under-served "now what" reduction-planning step. Own those two, at a long-tail price the Big 4 can't match, and the rest of the chain becomes a productisable wrapper around them.
