The chain-level insight

The UAE pressure curve is inverted relative to the UK.

In the UK, regulatory weight concentrates mid-to-late life — MEES gates the leasing of existing stock and UKSRS its disclosure, which is why the venture's UK thesis centres on the stranded existing asset. In the UAE, weight concentrates at stage 1 (design/permit, via the emirate green-building codes) and stage 8 (operational entity-level MRV, via the federal climate law) — with no federal in-life leasing ban in the middle. So the attachment points for a venture are the construction-documentation burden upstream and, far more scalably, the recurring occupier MRV obligation in operation.
