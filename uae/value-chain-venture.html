Where the venture itself sits: what it consumes, what it produces, how it reaches buyers, and what makes the position defensible. This is the business-model view — the synthesis of the wedge, the moat, the channels and the phased sequence from Module 8, drawn as one picture.

The core pipeline

Inputs / consumed

  • DEWA / ADDC grid emission factors
  • District-cooling data (Empower / Tabreed)
  • Refrigerant & fuel activity data
  • Free-zone & group entity structures
  • MOCCAE rules & IEQT submission specs
  • BMS / sub-meter / utility feeds
→

The engine (what the venture does)

  • UAE-CRE-localised MRV enginePre-loaded local factors, district-cooling logic, refrigerant modules, free-zone templates.
  • Reduction-plan generatorThe under-served "now what" — current + planned measures, expected results.
→

Outputs / produced

  • Defensible Scope 1/2 (→3) inventory
  • IEQT-submission-ready filing
  • Mandatory reduction plan
  • Verifier-ready evidence pack
  • Annual re-baseline & monitoring
The moat is not the technology. Any of these steps can be coded. The defensibility is UAE-CRE domain depth + localisation (the things generic Western SaaS gets wrong) + the India cost base (serving a long tail the Big 4 can't economically reach). Every feature is chosen to be one a generic tool can't replicate without UAE-CRE knowledge.

The phased sequence (how the chain is built over time)
Phase 0
0–6 mo
Credibility & first inventories — consulting
3–5 paying MRV engagements in Segment B, done by hand, anchored on the 30 May 2026 deadline. Goal: reference logos, real UAE-CRE data, discovery of which localisation problems recur. Revenue: consulting fees (funds the build).
Phase 1
6–18 mo
Productise the repeatable core
Turn the hand-built process into the localised MRV engine + reduction-plan generator. Sell low-touch to the long-tail volume market; keep higher-touch consulting for larger clients. Revenue: SaaS subscription begins (recurring).
Phase 2
12–24 mo
Anchor references & B2B2B channels
Win an anchor logo; open the consultancy-tooling and Big-4-referral channels. Revenue: B2B2B licensing + anchor contracts.
Phase 3
18–36 mo
The free-zone-authority platform prize ⚠ race condition
Pursue the Segment-D whale — but a funded incumbent (Plan A) is already reaching for it. Win on UAE-CRE localisation depth + timing, only once Phase 0–2 references give credibility.
Phase 4
24 mo+
Regional expansion (GCC)
The UAE is the beachhead for the Gulf — every neighbouring state is now mandating disclosure. The localised engine ports outward.
