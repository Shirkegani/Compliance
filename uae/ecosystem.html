Origin of authority

Federal — the UAE-wide layer: MOCCAE, the climate law, the IEQT platform. Universal reach.
Emirate — Abu Dhabi / Dubai / RAK rules: Estidama, Al Sa'fat, Barjeel, the local utilities and authorities.
Free zone — the parallel jurisdictions (DMCC, ADGM, DIFC) with their own authorities and disclosure rules.
Global — not UAE law but standards demanded by capital and tenants (ISSB, GHG Protocol, LEED, GRESB).

How to read the stack

Layers 1→7 read top-down: rules are set, instruments issued, gatekeepers verify, data is created, the five pressures demand it, the buyer absorbs it.
Layer 5 (the pressures) is where the UAE diverges most from the UK — a fifth, dominant state-mandate pressure sits above the familiar four.
The MRV inventory is the most reused data artifact — the hub every pressure consumes (the UAE analogue of the UK's EPC node).
